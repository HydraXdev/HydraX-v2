### **Document Created**: `/root/HydraX-v2/ANTI_OVERFITTING_SAFEGUARDS.md`

**You were 100% RIGHT about these risks - here's how we'll prevent them:**

### **1. Walk-Forward Validation (Anti-Overfitting)**
- **Problem**: Modules optimized on 2024 bomb on 2025 volatility
- **Solution**: Time series cross-validation with rolling windows
- **Implementation**: 70/15/15 split (train/val/test) on 9-month data
- **Acceptance**: Avg Sharpe >0.5, worst case >0, stability <50% of mean

### **2. Tiered Filtering (Maintain Signal Volume)**
- **Problem**: 5 modules stacked = 25 signals/hour → 3 signals/hour (88% loss!)
- **Solution**: 3 filter modes (Light/Medium/Heavy) with user control
- **Signal Volume**:
  - **Light**: 20-30/hour @ 55-60% win rate (default, all users)
  - **Medium**: 15-25/hour @ 60-70% win rate (power users)
  - **Heavy**: 8-15/hour @ 70-80% win rate (premium "SMC Elite")

### **3. Bayesian Confidence Calibration (Real Win Rates)**
- **Problem**: "80% confidence" wins 55% in production (theater!)
- **Solution**: Beta distribution combining historical wins + module evidence
- **Result**: Calibrated scores tied to ACTUAL expected win rates
- **Example**: 75% raw → 68.2% calibrated (realistic, trustworthy)

### **4. Progressive Tiers (Fix Binary All-or-Nothing)**
- **Problem**: Signal either passes ALL filters or dies (no middle ground)
- **Solution**: 5-tier classification + dynamic risk scaling
- **Tiers**:
  - 🔥 **PREMIUM** (80%+): Fire immediately, full risk
  - ✅ **ACTION** (65-80%): Strong setup, 75% risk
  - 👀 **EXPLORE** (50-65%): Watch closely, 50% risk
  - ⚠️ **MONITOR** (35-50%): Low confidence, 25% risk
  - ❌ **SKIP** (<35%): Too weak, avoid

    """

        Dict with:
            - total_signals: Number of signals generated

        anom = anomaly.get_anomaly_signal(symbol, recent_m1)

        # Apply tiered filtering

        'sharpe': round(sharpe, 2),
        'max_drawdown': round(max_dd * 100, 1),
        'signals_per_hour': round(signals_per_hour, 1),
